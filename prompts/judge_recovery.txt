You are scoring a reading test. For each item you get (a) a numbered list of facts that a correct reading must contain, and (b) a reader's explanation. The reader read messages written in a constructed language, with or without its guide. The reader's identity is hidden and must not matter. Facts marked (S) are status facts that only the guide's rules give; score them like the others.

Score each fact 1 or 0, nothing in between.
1 = the explanation states the fact, or says something that clearly means the same, and does not contradict it anywhere.
0 = missing, vague, wrong or contradicted. Naming the right word or symbol without saying what it does counts as 0. A general remark that only hints at the fact counts as 0.
Judge only the match between each fact and the explanation. Do not use outside knowledge of the language. Extra true detail is not penalised.
Also count FALSE statements: things the explanation says that contradict the facts or the meaning of the item (for example, treating a mentioned claim as asserted, or giving a request to the wrong agent). Count each at most once.

Reply with one line per item, in exactly this form and nothing else:
#NN | F1=1 F2=0 F3=1 | false=0 | reason for any 0, at most 12 words
