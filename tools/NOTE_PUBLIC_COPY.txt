Public copy of the study's working tools.

These are the scripts as they were used: they build the packets and judge sheets, file and parse the replies, check the judges' replies,
score Test F and make the tables. They are published so that every step can be read. Most of them cannot be run from this package alone,
because they read the frozen kit (items, answer keys, packets), the models' replies or the judge sheets, and these are withheld (see ../README.md).

Paths such as /home/claude/silex_results and /home/claude/silex_blind_test are the runner's work folders and serve as defaults; pass your own
with the options each tool describes. The tools run with python3 -I. README.txt, README_E.txt and README_RT.txt are the working notes, written
during the study; they speak of RES (the results folder) and KIT (the frozen kit).

Files are copies of the working files as they stood on the evening of 14 November 2026. Nothing was edited for publication. The file hashes are in ../SHA256SUMS.
